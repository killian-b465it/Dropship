🗺️ Your Setup Roadmap
Follow these steps to launch your dropshipping store
1
Create Your Shopify Store
Sign up for Shopify's free trial. Choose a store name, pick a theme, and set up your payment gateway (Shopify Payments or PayPal).
Start Free →
2
Install a Supplier App
Install DSers (free) or AutoDS to connect AliExpress/CJ Dropshipping to your store. This automates order fulfillment.
Browse Suppliers →
3
Research Winning Products
Use DropLaunch to find products with high demand, low competition, and good margins. Save them to your store list.
Find Products →
4
Import Products to Shopify
Use DSers or AutoDS to import your chosen products to Shopify. Write compelling descriptions and set your sell price.
Import Guide →
5
Set Up Facebook/TikTok Ads
Create a Facebook Business account and TikTok Ads account. Study winning ads in the Ad Library, then create your first campaign.
View Ad Library →
6
Track Orders & Scale
Monitor orders in the Orders tab, track profits in Analytics, and double down on your best-performing products.
View Analytics →
